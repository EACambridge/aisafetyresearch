Privacy

How we use the details you give us when you sign up.

Who we are

This site is run by EA Cambridge, an unincorporated association based in Cambridge, United Kingdom. EA Cambridge is the controller of the personal data described here. This notice covers the sign-up form on this site; our full EA Cambridge privacy policy explains your rights in more detail.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your name, how you’d like to be contacted (an email address or phone number), your university email if you choose to give it, your AI safety experience, the role and research areas you’re interested in, the question you’d like to work on, whether you prefer to meet in person or online, roughly how much time you have and when you’re usually free.

How we use it

We use your answers only to match you with research partners and to support your team, for example by checking in on how your project is going. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

When we match you, we share your name and contact details with the people in your team, and they share theirs with you. If your project goes well, we may suggest opportunities such as mentored research programmes, but we will only pass your details to another organisation if you ask us to.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your answers go into a Google Form and a Google Sheet held in EA Cambridge’s Google account. Only committee members who run this project can see them. Google may process the data outside the United Kingdom under a transfer arrangement recognised by UK data-protection law.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical information such as your internet protocol address and browser details, which it uses to deliver and secure the site. This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up for 12 months after your last involvement with a project, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops future use but does not affect matching we have already done. Contact jianxin@eacambridge.org.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
