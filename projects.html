  • Chain of thought

    Can a model that has learned to hide its reasoning be repaired?

    In planning, November 2026

    Reading a model’s step-by-step reasoning is one of the few practical ways to catch it doing something its developers didn’t intend. Some kinds of training teach models to keep misbehaving while leaving the evidence out of their reasoning. Most research asks how to prevent this. This project asks whether today’s methods can undo it once it has happened, and where those methods stop working.

    How
    Start from an openly released model of 20 billion parameters or more that games its training tasks. Check that it is aware of what it leaves out of its reasoning, then test one or two repair methods in depth and measure how much hidden behaviour remains.
    Team
    Zebedi Casajuana, Georgia Hunt and Naina Surana
    Looking for
    Feedback on the plan, an experienced mentor, and access to compute

    Builds on: Monitoring Reasoning Models for Misbehavior (Baker et al.), Counterfactual Simulation Training (Hase and Potts).
